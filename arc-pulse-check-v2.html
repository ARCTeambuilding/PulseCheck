Every high-intensity work cycle leaves behind "Communication Debt"—the minor frictions and work-arounds that accumulate when we prioritize output over alignment.

This three-minute protocol is designed to help your team name what shifted during your most recent peak period and begin the process of a deliberate reset.

Question 1 of 3 The Delta Check

The Delta Check

"On a scale of 1 to 10, how much did our communication style change under the pressure of the last 60 days?"
Facilitator note — private
Let the number settle before asking the follow-up. Silence here is telling — it often means people are calibrating whether it's safe to be honest. Scores of 7 or higher typically signal significant accumulated debt.

Question 2 of 3 The Work-Around Audit

The Work-Around Audit

"What is one 'unspoken rule' or workaround we created to navigate the recent crunch that we should consciously retire now?"
Facilitator note — private
This question tends to unlock the most candid responses. Go first with a low-stakes example to lower the barrier — it gives permission for honesty. Probe gently if needed: "Did we start skipping any steps, meetings, or conversations we used to value?"
Question 3 of 3 The Orientation Inquiry

The Orientation Inquiry

"If we could re-map how we collaborate for the coming quarter, what is one personality trait or work-style in a teammate you would like to understand better?"
Facilitator note — private
Frame this as curiosity, not critique. The goal is to surface invisible friction between working styles — not assign blame. You may hear things like "I'd like to understand why someone goes quiet under pressure." These are the seeds of real recalibration.
